We are offering a fully funded 3-year PhD studentship on [Trustworthy Generative AI in Healthcare](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/dentistry/research/phd-opportunities-folder/cocts-trustworthy-generative-ai-in-healthcare), supervised by [Yunpeng Li](https://yunpengli.ac/) and [Yali Du](https://yalidu.github.io/). The award covers fees at the home or international rate plus a stipend at the UKRI London rate, for a February, June or October 2027 start. Applications are assessed on a rolling basis (Ref: 2026YL-TGAi-R2). See the [Openings](/openings/) page.
