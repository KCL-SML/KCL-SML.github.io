TAIR joins [BioFACTORy](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/news/new-european-project-to-improve-bone-regenerative-therapy), a new Horizon Europe project building a fully automated, AI-powered platform for manufacturing stem cell-based bioimplants for bone regeneration. The consortium brings together 14 partners from 9 countries, coordinated by the University of Bergen. [Yunpeng Li](https://yunpengli.ac/) leads the AI module at King's, with [Owen Addison](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/owen-addison) and Shuntaro Yamada.
