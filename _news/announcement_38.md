Our Horizon Europe project [BioFACTORy](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/news/new-european-project-to-improve-bone-regenerative-therapy) has started. Coordinated by the University of Bergen, it brings together 14 partners across nine countries to build an automated, AI-powered platform for manufacturing stem cell-based bioimplants for craniofacial bone repair.
